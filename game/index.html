Watson Dog

You're Watson, a brown dog with a big heart and a bigger bite. The townsfolk have had enough of you, and they're coming at you with newspapers, parcels, rakes and nets. Chase them off to earn experience (XP), then spend it at your doghouse in the middle of town on new levels, new fetch gear and new tricks. The farther you roam from home, the tougher the townsfolk, and the more XP they're worth.

This game is meant to be hard. People flash and show a red circle on the ground before they swing. Roll out of the circle or get hit. When you're knocked out, all your unspent XP is left where you fell (look for the glowing bone). Get back to it to recover it. Get knocked out on the way and it's gone for good.

Attacks, rolls, running and moves all use stamina (the green bar). Run out and you can't attack or roll. Attacking commits you: you can't roll until the chomp finishes, so mashing will get you knocked out.

Punish openings. Right after someone swings at you, they're briefly off balance and a yellow ring appears under it. Hits landed then are critical hits for double damage.
