The Indomitable Fires of the Bloodwars

Kill monsters to earn experience (XP). Spend it at the fire in the middle of the map to level up your stats, or on new weapons and moves. The farther you go from the fire, the deadlier the monsters, and the more XP they're worth.

This game is meant to be hard. Enemies flash and show a red circle on the ground before they strike. Roll out of the circle or get hit. When you die, all your unspent XP is left where you fell. Get back to it to recover it. Die on the way and it's gone for good.
